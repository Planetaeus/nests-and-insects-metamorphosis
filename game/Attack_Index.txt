
/h1 Attacks

In Combat, Attacking is the main way to inflict Wounds upon Enemy Insects. Each Insect can know up to four attacks at once, and can only choose Attacks for which she Qualifies, which is to say that she meets its Requirements.

This section describes a number of pre-made Attacks for the game. The Game Queen is free to work with her players to create new attacks if she wishes.

The list of available Attacks is available in Chapter "Attacks". Detailed descriptions of these attacks are available in Attack_Index.txt.

/h2 Reading Attacks

Attacks have four Attributes: Name, Range, Requirements, and Description. The following sections speak about these 

/h3 Attack Names

Each Attack has a name that is used to distinguish it from other attacks. The name is usually no longer than two words.

When coming up with your own Attacks, do not forget to name them.

/h3 Attack Ranges

* Melee: Melee Attacks require the Attacker to be Adjacent to the target of the Attack.

* Ranged: Ranged attacks specify a maximum range they can target opponents from. Ranged attacks cannot target Adjacent Insects.

* Area: Affects all Insects within a specified area.

/h3 Attack Description Glossary

*Adjacent: The Insect or Hex is touching the Hex of interest, typically the Attacker or a chosen Hex.

* Gains X for each Net Success: Represents an amount of Wounds or some Effect that is bestowed upon an Insect. This phrase may have more than one component, such as "gains Wounds and Immobile for each Net Success". In this case, all of the listed components are bestowed in the same amount.

* Pushed X: The Insect is forcibly moved against its will X Hexes. If unspecified, the direction is directly away from the Attacker. It may be specified as directly away from a chosen Hex ("Pushed X from chosen Hex"), a direction of your choice ("Pushed X in any direction"), or directly towards the Attacker ("Pushed X towards you")

* Within X Hexes: X represents a set number, or a Rating. A Hex is within X Hexes if it takes no more than X Hex-steps to move to that Hex.

/h2 Attack Descriptions

Armor Bash
Range: Close
Requires: Carapace 1
Roll STR + Carapace vs. STR + Carapace against an adjacent Insect. For each Net Success, the target gains Wounds and an adjacent Ally gains a positive modifier to her next Attack Resistance Roll.

Armored Charge
Range: Area
Requires: Carapace 1
Move in a line of Hexes up to your SPE. Roll SPE - Swarming. Each Insect in that line must roll SPE + Carapace to Resist, then gains Wounds and is Pushed 1 for each Net Success.

Armored Spikes
Range: Special
Requires: Carapace 1
The next time you are Missed by a Close Attack before your next turn, the Attacker gains Wounds and Bleeding for each Net Failure

Assuréd Strike
Range: Close
Requires: KEN 7
Roll KEN against an adjacent Insect. The target gains Wounds for each Net Success.

Autothysis
Range: Area
Requires: Swarming 1
Spend one Swarms and Roll KEN + Swarming and choose a Hex within Swarming Hexes. Each Insect within one Hex rolls SPE - Swarming to Resist, then gains Wounds and Immobile for each Net Success.

Bandage
Range: Close
Requires: Web 1, Healing 1
Roll Web + Healing vs. STR against an adjacent Ally. For each Net Success, target heals Wounds and reduces Bleeding.

Bolster
Range: Close
Requires: Leadership 1
Roll PAS + Leadership vs. PAS + Carapace against an adjacent Insect. The target gains Wounds for each Net Success. Then each Ally within Leadership Hexes Rolls as if Resisting a Rally Action, then Advances appropriately.

Bright Beam
Range: Ranged
Requires: Light 1

Burning Blaze

Chitin Catapult
